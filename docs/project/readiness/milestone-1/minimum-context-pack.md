# Milestone 1 Minimum Context Pack

Every milestone-one role receives only the material required by its assignment:

- `AGENTS.md` and its own `.codex/agents/<role>.toml` role contract;
- the approved project specification and approved plan;
- the milestone-one section and its owned task/work-package brief;
- the frozen milestone-one architecture contract and relevant interface examples;
- the milestone readiness/contract-snapshot references and current safe-resume marker;
- prior-task handoff evidence only when it is a declared dependency.

Implementers do not receive reviewer or auditor instructions as authority to self-approve. Reviewers receive the exact candidate diff/evidence package but do not mutate it. The Independent Auditor reports only to the Orchestrator.
